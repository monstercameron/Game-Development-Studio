Built for people and agents

Your studio,
at your agent’s fingertips.

Forge exposes its creative workflows through the Model Context Protocol. An agent can discover models, organize project assets, queue renders, check progress, and help diagnose the studio.

✳

DiscoverInspect models, inputs, services and hardware.
